## Operators

### Most Common
- Expression Grouping & Function Call (`()`)
- Arithmetic (`+`,`-`,`*`,`/`,`%`,`++`,`--`,`**`)
- Comparison (`<`,`>`,`==`,`!=`,`<=`,`>=`,`===`,`!==`)
- Concatenation (`+`,`[]`)
- Logical (`&&`,`||`,`!`,`!!`)
- Assignment (`=`,`:`,`=>`,compound)
- Accessing (`[]`,`.`)
- Comma (`,`)
- 

### Less Common
- Flow Control (`||`,`&&`,`??`,`?:`,`yield`,`yield*`)
- Bitwise (`&`,`|`,`~`,`^`,`>>`,`<<`,`>>>`)
- Unary (`+`,`-`,`delete`,`typeof`,`void`)
- Relational (`in`,`instanceof`)
- Optional Chaining (`?.`,`?.[]`)
- Spread/Rest (`...`)
- 

## Literals
- Booleans (`true`,`false`)
- Numeric (`0b`,`0`,`0o`,`0x`,`e`,`E`,`.`,`n`)
- Regular & Template Strings (`""`,`''`,<code>``</code>)
- Objects (`{}`,`:`,`.`,`?.`,`[""]`,`?.[""]`,`null`)
- Arrays (`[]`)
- Functions (`() {}`,`=>`)
- Regular Expression (`//`)
